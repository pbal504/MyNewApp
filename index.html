    Spin it yourself

    Everyone on the platform turns at the same angular speed, but riders farther from the center travel a bigger circle, so they move faster and need more force to stay on course.

    Watch the path: with nothing pulling inward, the rider keeps going in a straight line along the direction they were moving. That is Newton's first law, inertia.

    Gear Lab

    Why do the horses gallop?

    Imagine cutting the stationary crown gear at the top of the center pole and laying it out flat. As the ride turns, each crank's little pinion gear rolls along it, so the crank spins and lifts the horse up and down.
